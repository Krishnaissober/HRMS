P174: 26.3.2 Recommended Application Architecture

P178: File flow: Client upload → validated upload endpoint → private object storage → document metadata stored in PostgreSQL.
P179: The system SHALL keep candidate attendance and employee attendance as separate business concepts, even if they share infrastructure and reporting primitives.

P264: 1. Introduction
P265: 1.1 Purpose
P266: This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the requirements for a web-based Human Resources Management and Employee Lifecycle Portal. The platform is intended to provide HR teams with a single system to capture, manage, track, approve, report on, and audit employment-related workflows from initial candidate contact through hiring, employment operations, and employee exit.
P267: The SRS is written as an implementation baseline for product, UX, frontend, backend, database, QA, security, and deployment teams. It separates mandatory functional requirements from future enhancements and identifies key entities, workflows, permissions, data, interfaces, and non-functional expectations.
P268: 1.2 Product Definition
P269: The product combines HRMS and ATS capabilities. It has two interconnected domains: (1) Recruitment / Candidate Lifecycle and (2) Employee Lifecycle / HR Operations. A candidate can be converted into an employee while preserving approved information and maintaining an auditable relationship between the candidate application, interview decisions, offer, and resulting employee record.

P377: 4.1 Permission Model
P378: Permissions SHALL be action-based (view, create, update, approve, export, download, delete/archive) and resource-based.
P379: Sensitive attributes such as salary, bank data, government IDs and confidential documents SHALL support field/document-level access rules where required.

P381: Approval permissions SHALL be distinguishable from edit permissions.
P382: Export/download access SHALL be independently permissioned because downloads can create data leakage risk.

P424: 7. Forms and Candidate Intake
P425: 7.1 Online Hiring Form
P426: HR shall create a job-linked public application form.
P427: The form shall collect configurable personal, contact, address, education, employment, skills, expected compensation, notice period, source, declaration and consent data.
P428: The form shall support resume/CV and supporting document uploads subject to validation rules.
P429: The application URL SHALL be publicly shareable without exposing administrative screens.
P430: Submitted applications SHALL automatically create or match a candidate and create an application against the selected job/requisition.
P431: The form shall support required fields, validation, conditional fields and anti-abuse protection.
P432: A candidate submission confirmation SHALL be displayed and optionally emailed.
P433: 7.2 Walk-in Form
P434: HR/reception shall create a candidate record through an internal walk-in form.
P435: The form shall capture candidate identity, contact details, role of interest, experience, source, resume and visit details.
P436: The system SHALL support candidate check-in and check-out associated with the walk-in visit.
P437: A printable/downloadable copy SHALL be available after submission.
P438: HR SHALL be able to convert a walk-in record into a normal application against an open requisition without re-entering the candidate data.
P439: 7.3 Form Download / Print
P440: Both the online hiring form submission and walk-in form submission SHALL be rendered into a consistent printable format.
P441: Authorized users SHALL be able to download PDF copies of the submitted form.
P442: Downloaded PDFs SHALL contain form metadata, submission date/time and a unique submission/reference number.
P443: Document download events SHALL be audited when the form contains sensitive data.

P584: 23. Compliance, Audit and Security
P585: All protected API endpoints SHALL enforce authentication and authorization server-side.
P586: Tenant/organization context SHALL be derived from the authenticated session or token and verified against the requested record.
P587: Sensitive data SHALL be encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and protected at rest using platform-appropriate encryption controls.
P588: Passwords SHALL be stored using strong, salted password hashing; raw passwords SHALL never be persisted or logged.
P589: The application SHALL validate uploaded file type/size and should validate file signatures where appropriate to prevent malicious uploads.
P590: Audit logs SHALL record actor, action, resource, timestamp, organization, request correlation ID where available, and outcome.
P591: Administrative and sensitive actions should require elevated permission and may require re-authentication or MFA depending on policy.
P592: Bulk exports SHALL be permission-controlled and auditable.
P593: Logs SHALL avoid storing unnecessary personal or secret data.
P594: Data retention and deletion behavior SHALL be configurable and aligned with the organization’s legal/policy requirements.
P595: The system SHALL support configurable session timeout, account lock/rate limiting and suspicious login monitoring.
P596: Security controls SHALL be applied consistently across UI, API, background jobs, exports and integrations.
